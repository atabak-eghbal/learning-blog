## Summary
- Hash maps are the go-to for counting, membership, and “seen before” logic.
- Often converts O(n^2) into O(n).

## Common patterns
- Frequency table (counts)
- First occurrence index
- Complement lookup (two-sum style)

## Pitfalls
- Confusing “missing key” vs “key with value 0”
- Updating counts in the wrong order
