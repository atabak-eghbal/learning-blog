I’m building a structured learning blog and revisiting topics via spaced repetition.

Each post follows a consistent format: a summary, the core ideas I can explain in my own words, common pitfalls, a mini-quiz, and a note on when to revisit it next. The goal is to make learning stick — not just to write things down once and forget them.

Use the navigation links at the top to browse posts by topic area. Categories include DSA, ML Fundamentals, MLOps, AI Engineering, Agentic AI, Math, System Design, and Projects.

If you’re here from LinkedIn: welcome — feel free to browse by category in the top navigation.
